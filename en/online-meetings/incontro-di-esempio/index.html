A sample page showing how the association’s online meetings will work.

Sample page: this meeting is not real and the button does not lead to any meeting room.

This page shows how online meetings will be presented. Each event has its own page with an introduction, the guest, the date and the time.

The “Join” button becomes active fifteen minutes before the start and takes you straight into the video meeting, with no registration and no software to install. With “Add to Calendar” the meeting goes into the calendar on your phone or computer, with a reminder.

After the meeting, if the guest agrees, the recording can be published here.
