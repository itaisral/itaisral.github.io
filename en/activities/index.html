What We Focus On

Our Areas of Work

Fuori Pagina

Some books make you want to keep the conversation going. We bring them to the table with their authors, at an osteria in Alessandria, for the pleasure of a conversation that can last all evening.

Music, Art and Exhibitions

Concerts, exhibitions and performances with Jewish and Israeli artists, to share a culture that is creating something new every day.

Online Meetings

Remote conversations with scholars, journalists and witnesses, including live from Israel.

Schools and Young People

Programs for students and teachers, and exchanges between young Italians and Israelis in education, culture and sports.

Travel and Exchanges

Trips to Israel to get to know the country up close, and exchanges with its cities and organizations.

Jewish History of Alessandria
