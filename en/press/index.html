The Association in Brief

The Associazione Italia Israele • Alessandria promotes knowledge, dialogue and cooperation between Italy and the State of Israel, with the aim of strengthening the cultural, political and social ties that unite the two countries. Founded through the commitment of twenty-two founding members, it resumed its activities in 2026, is open to anyone who shares its principles and aims, and is affiliated with the Unione delle Associazioni Italia Israele, the national union of Italy–Israel associations.

Combating antisemitism, in whatever form it takes and from whatever quarter it comes, is an essential part of its work, together with opposing anti-Jewish prejudice, the falsification of history and every attempt to justify violence against Jews.

The association will host “Fuori pagina,” evenings with authors around the table of a local osteria, online conversations with scholars, journalists and witnesses, some of them live from Israel, and programs for schools and young people. It fosters relations with institutions and cultural exchanges between the two countries, and works on the Jewish history of Alessandria, on the families and individuals who contributed to the life of the city and on the heritage they left behind. In 2026 it organized the commemoration of the third anniversary of October 7 in front of the synagogue on Via Milano.

Its logo, inspired by Futurism, brings together the symbols of Italy and Israel around the Meier Bridge, an emblem of the contemporary city.
