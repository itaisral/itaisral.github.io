The Logo

One Mark, Three Identities

The logo brings together references to Italy, to Israel and to the city of Alessandria in a composition inspired by the visual language of Futurism. The choice of shapes, directions and color relationships expresses a dynamic view of the relationship between the two countries, founded on encounter, cultural exchange and the building of lasting ties.

The Futurist inspiration lies above all in the treatment of movement and compositional tension. The diagonals, the tapered shapes and the arrangement of the elements along diverging axes give the mark a recognizable momentum. The reference to this artistic language reflects the wish to represent an association that is active in the present and oriented toward the future, and whose roots in history sustain its cultural and civic commitment.

The three blades in the colors of the Italian flag take the form of trails, suggesting acceleration and continuous movement. The tricolor thus keeps its identifying role while becoming part of the structure of the logo through a concise and dynamic representation.

The blue introduces the reference to Israel and organizes an essential part of the composition. It acts as an axis of force and a trajectory, connecting the elements and sustaining their balance. The Star of David keeps an autonomous position and remains fully recognizable, making the reference to Jewish and Israeli identity explicit.

The connection with Alessandria is entrusted to two complementary elements. The Meier Bridge, distilled into the arched line, refers to the city’s landscape and introduces the theme of connection. Its symbolic role evokes the meeting of communities, experiences and cultural traditions; formally, the arch balances the thrust of the diagonals and helps hold the composition together.

The coat of arms of Alessandria represents the civic and local core of the logo. Its presence identifies the place where the association’s work takes shape and where the friendship between Italy and Israel finds concrete public expression.

The composition thus brings three recognizable identities into relation, preserving what is specific to each and placing them within a unified system. The movement of the shapes, the connecting role of the bridge and the rootedness expressed by the coat of arms translate into visual form the principles that guide the association: cultural dialogue, mutual knowledge, cooperation founded on civic responsibility, and the development of forward-looking relations between Italy and Israel.
