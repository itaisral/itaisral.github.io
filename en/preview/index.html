About Us

A Bridge Between Alessandria and Israel

We promote knowledge, dialogue and cooperation between Italy and the State of Israel, to strengthen the cultural, political and social ties that unite the two countries.

The bridge in our logo is Richard Meier’s bridge over the Tanaro River. We chose it because it captures what we do. What the logo means.

October 7, 2023

Remembering, with Sources

On October 7, 2023, more Jews were killed than on any other day since the end of the Holocaust.

On the third anniversary we gathered again in front of the synagogue on Via Milano to remember the victims and the hostages. On the dedicated page we have collected the voices of those who were there, the documents and a map of the people and places.
