Holidays, Fasts and Shabbat

Today’s Hebrew date, Shabbat in Alessandria and the holidays of the next twelve months.

Holidays begin at sundown the evening before and end at nightfall on the last day. Dates follow Diaspora practice, as observed by the Italian Jewish communities, and Shabbat candle lighting is given 18 minutes before sunset. All times are Italian time. For other cities, see Hebcal.
