About Us

A Bridge Between Alessandria and Israel

We are an association of citizens who care about the bond between Italy and the State of Israel.

What Drives Us

The Associazione Italia Israele of Alessandria promotes knowledge, dialogue and cooperation between Italy and the State of Israel, with the aim of strengthening the cultural, political and social ties that unite the two countries. Through meetings, cultural exchanges and initiatives that also involve young people, we build bridges between our city and Israel.

Fighting antisemitism is an essential part of our commitment, whatever form it takes and wherever it comes from. We oppose anti-Jewish prejudice, the falsification of history and every attempt to justify violence against Jews.

Alessandria

Here, this commitment also means learning about local Jewish history, recovering the stories of the people and families who contributed to the life of the city, and honoring the heritage they left us.

Alongside history, we look for opportunities to encounter contemporary Jewish and Israeli culture through people, ideas, literature and the arts. Knowledge helps people move beyond superficial portrayals and deep-rooted prejudice.

How We Work

We cultivate relationships with institutions, cultural and educational exchanges, and initiatives for young Italians and Israelis in schools, culture, sports and civic life. We bring books to the table together with their authors, and we organize online meetings and programs for schools.

The association was founded through the commitment of twenty-two founding members. Today it is open to anyone who shares its principles and aims.

The Logo

Our logo brings together Italy, Israel and Alessandria in a composition inspired by Futurism. We explain its meaning on a dedicated page.
